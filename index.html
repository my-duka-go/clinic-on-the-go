Clinic profile

This is the clinic's own details - name, owner, contact and location. It's what prints on receipts and shows in the header, separate from the Clinic On the Go app itself.

Branding - make it yours

Your own logo and background tint, just for this clinic - synced to every device signed into it. This is separate from clinic type, which stays with your platform administrator.


How staff join this clinic

There's no code to hand out - a staff member's own PIN is their identity. Add them under Staff below with their name, role, and a PIN, then tell them that PIN. They can type it into this same app on their own phone, anywhere, anytime - the app finds this clinic automatically and pulls everything in.

This desk

Add staff

Each person needs their own PIN - checked as unique across every clinic on the platform, not just this one. Give someone their PIN and they can sign in with it on their own phone, anywhere, and the app finds this clinic automatically.

Roles, in short

Nurse - registers patients, checks them in and takes payment; 4-digit PIN. Removing a line or clearing a bill is never blocked, but it's sent to Admin → Removals for an administrator or doctor to review afterward.
Doctor - everything a nurse can do, plus can remove items or clear a bill straight away (no review needed, still fully logged) and approve what nurses send for review.
Administrator - full access: Stock, Reports, Sync setup, staff and everyone's role. Administrator PINs are 6 digits - longer on purpose, so they are harder to pick up by watching the keypad.

Online / offline behavior

      
This isn't a toggle you manage day to day - it's automatic.

      

        The app watches the device's real network state. Lose signal mid-bill and nothing changes for the
        nurse - bills keep ringing up, stock keeps deducting, everything queues locally. The moment the
        connection is back, queued bills and stock updates drain to the backend on their own. The button below
        is only there to test that flow on demand, without physically switching off wifi.
        


        One thing to set up once you host this for real: the page itself needs to survive a reload with zero
        network too - that's what manifest.json
        and sw.js (included alongside
        this file) handle. Host all three together and open the app once while online - after that, it opens
        offline, cold, with no connection at all.
